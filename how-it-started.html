How It Started

Ours started a little earlier than that.

5th Grade | 2022-2023

Before I Even Knew You

You lived in my house before I did. Very funny lol

I hadn't even seen your face. I barely knew who you were.

And somehow, I still fell for you somehow. I ended up falling for your passion and I guess weirdness? I was considered really weird and wasn't in the best mental state and I think seeing someone so carefree and funny made me fall HARD I remember just thinking it was a small crush and nothing more... Another crush that'll fade Turns out that was NOT the case

6th Grade | 2023-2024

I Finally Met You

Then I moved to Orinda.

Suddenly, you weren't just someone I had heard about anymore. You were actually there IRL BRO.

I started following you around, defiently not ethically or normally since when was I ever normal? Eventually we even did archery together. Story is around this time I had a crush on Katy, Luc, Dason, and you. All of them were "small" crushes and not like a poly thing but I remember just kinda stalking all of yall One silly confession I have is when I was following you while you were talking to this boy and you turned back and made eye contact with me and I started to stare at the ground Is there ANY significance to that? Hell nah but I remember that eye contact made me lose it and I spent the entire week thinking and writing about you lmao

And the more I knew you, ethically or not, the more I fell.

7th Grade | 2024-2025

I Tried To Let Go

I liked you for a few months.

But whenever I was with someone else, I stopped letting myself like you.

Because if I was with someone, I wanted to be loyal to them. And to be fair, I was. I do everything a good girlfriend did but I was VERY mentally ill I tried so hard to be perfect but of course, No relationships worked out

And every time those relationships ended, somehow my feelings for you came back. Whether within a week or month. It always came back to you. Today, I am glad those relationships didn't work out.

8th Grade (ignore how its kinda 9th as well) | 2025-2026

I Fell Harder

In 8th grade, I fell for you again. We had yearbook and PE and I just was so happy when I liked you. I remember looking at you and thinking you were so handsome and funny and fell HARD

But I ended up getting together with someone else. I don't know whether I regret it or not since this boy is not a bad person This someone treated me very well. I genuinely did like him but I knew my mental health will make this end sooner or later (sorry jayden)

Then we broke up. As expected. I was crushed since he was one of the only people holding me together. I remember getting drunk and struggling to move on.

And of course, you were there. You comforted me when I needed someone. I did have my two closest friends and I am truly grateful to them but the way you comforted me... Java makes me laugh when I'm sad and Axel hypes me up but YOU? You make me feel like I matter. You make me feel like I won't die at 15. You make me feel like a starstruck girl in love.

That's when I realized I couldn't keep running from how I felt.

9th Grade | 2026-HOPEFULLY 2027

"Octaviia"

I was kinda a coward on how I handled this. I was sobbing on the bathroom floor after our talk when you called and comforted me But I wasn't crying over Jayden at all (well partially) I was crying because this time the feelings were MUCH MORE stronger I never have felt this strongly in love with you and I GENUINELY Did NOT know what to do So I ignored my feelings for you for a bit After all, I was only sorta over Jayden or beginning to because of you It wasnt till early September I decided to finally show myself And instead of saying everything out loud, I started talking to you as a nonchalant mysterious entity

With...Discord!

One after another, I kept leaving little pieces of what I felt without telling you who they were from. I had a feeling you were gonna eventually find out. And I was fine with that but also scared. However I couldn't let these 4 year long feelings marinate anymore

Until eventually, I finally told you. Even though you kinda already knew-

September 25

The Mid-Autumn Festival

Around 50 hours of work.

I don't regret a second.

If that got you to say yes I won't EVER regret confessing to you. I wished I could've done more though but that day is the day we got together!

I remember planning this with Java and making it perfect! I remember making Charles Gall read it over and watch him cringe I remember spending every morning reciting what to say and ending up running away flustered But still, ZERO regrets

And maybe that's the best part. After all those years, we finally made it here.

And that's how we got here

A story that started before I even knew your face somehow became a story about us. (wow so poetic)

And I think there's still more to write :)
